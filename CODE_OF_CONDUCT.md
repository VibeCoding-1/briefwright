# Code of conduct

This project is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, or anywhere they speak for the project — keeps to this code.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Criticise the work, never the person, and explain what would make it better.
- Accept that a decision may go against you, and say so calmly if you disagree.
- Help newcomers find their way; nobody starts out knowing the conventions.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, or sexual content.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their consent.
- Repeatedly derailing a discussion, or pressing someone who has asked you to stop.

## Reporting

Report a breach privately to the maintainers through the repository's private reporting: open the **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with "Conduct report". Only the maintainers can read it, and they keep the reporter's identity confidential. For content on GitHub you can also use GitHub's own abuse reporting.

## What happens next

The maintainers review every report and reply. Depending on what happened, they may ask for a change, edit or remove a contribution, give a warning, or block someone from the project for a time or for good. A maintainer involved in a report takes no part in deciding it.
